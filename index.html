FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is a faceless video?

A video where nobody appears on camera: the story is told with narration, animation and on-screen text. Many of the biggest history, science, finance and sports channels work this way.

Do I need editing skills?

No. You can go from a topic to a finished video without touching a timeline. The editor is there when you want more control.

How long does one video take?

Minutes of your time instead of days. Claude writes and builds while you watch, then your Mac makes the file: in our tests a one-minute 3D video was ready in under three minutes on a MacBook Pro, and simpler 2D styles are quicker.

Can I really make money with faceless videos?

Many channels do, but results are never certain. Earnings depend on your niche, your audience and how consistently you post, and platforms have their own entry rules (YouTube needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours or 10 million Shorts views). The calculator on this page shows illustrative ranges, not promises.

How much does it cost?

Two plans: Creator at $69 a month for solo creators, and Studio at $129 a month for agencies, client work and many channels. One freelance-edited video costs about $120, so the plan pays for itself with your first video. Pay annually and get two months free. Cancel any time.

Will it work on my Mac?

Zaga Studio runs on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). It opens in under a second and works offline. Windows is coming; join the newsletter to hear first.

What do I need for the AI parts?

Zaga uses your own Claude account for writing and editing. Voices are included; premium voices from ElevenLabs or Fish Audio work with your own account if you want them.

Are the videos mine to monetize?

Yes. Platforms reward original work, so bring your own angle and cite your sources. Zaga draws new scenes for every script instead of reposting other people's clips.

Is voice cloning allowed?

Only with consent. Zaga clones a voice only when the person it belongs to has agreed.
